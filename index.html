Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A focused set of features designed to make everyday browsing fast, clean, and completely under your control.

Multi-tab browsing

Open as many tabs as you need. Switch, duplicate, and close tabs with a tap. A clean tab overview keeps everything under control.

Private browsing

Open private tabs that don't save history, cookies, or form data between sessions. Clean, isolated, and easy to close.

Smart address bar

One field for everything. Type a URL or a search query — Zishu detects which one you mean and takes you straight there.

Bookmarks & history

Save pages you care about and find past visits quickly. Both stored locally, both under your control, both easy to clear.

Built-in download manager

Track progress, speed, and remaining time. Pause, cancel, retry, or open finished files — all from a clean downloads screen.

Desktop site mode

Request the full desktop version of any website whenever you want a wider layout on a smaller screen.

Complete settings panel

Search engine, homepage, theme, zoom, JavaScript, popups, autoplay, cookies, and site permissions — all in one place.

Site permissions

Decide when sites can use the camera, microphone, location, and notifications. Nothing is ever granted silently.

Find in page

Search for text inside any page and jump between matches with next and previous navigation. Fast and clean.

Privacy & Control

Your browsing, on your terms

Zishu Browser gives you practical controls — not marketing promises. Choose what stays on your device and clear what you don't need.

Private browsing mode

A separate mode for sessions that shouldn't leave a trace in your regular history.

Cookie controls

Allow or block cookies. Clear them any time from Settings.

Do Not Track

Send a Do Not Track signal to sites that respect it.

HTTPS preference

Prefer secure connections and warn when a site's certificate can't be verified.

Site permissions

Control camera, microphone, location, and notification access per site.

Clear browsing data

Remove history, cookies, cache, and site data whenever you choose.

Privacy Policy

How Zishu Browser handles your data.

Summary

Zishu Browser does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal browsing data. Browsing history, bookmarks, downloads, and settings are stored locally on your device. You can clear them at any time from Settings.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Your browsing history
  • Your search queries
  • Your bookmarks
  • Your location
  • Your personal files
  • Personally identifiable information

What Is Stored Locally

  • Browsing history (if enabled)
  • Bookmarks
  • Download records
  • Browser settings and preferences
  • Quick links

Permissions

Zishu Browser requests permissions only to provide functionality — internet to browse, storage to save downloads, and camera/microphone/location only when you allow a site to use them. You can revoke any permission from Android Settings.

Third-Party Services

Zishu Browser does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
